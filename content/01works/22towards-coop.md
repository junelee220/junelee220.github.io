towards COOP（在一起合作社）是武汉朋友们发起的一系列集体行动，从2022年春节的"路上行舟"开始，延续到2023年的"过年七天乐"和2025年的"顺流而下"。这些行动以集体身体实践为核心，在城市公共空间中创造相聚和连结的可能——抬船上街、野趴露营、划船顺流而下——每一次行动都是对"在一起"这一最基本政治姿态的 rehearsing。

towards COOP is a series of collective actions initiated by friends in Wuhan, starting from "Go to street with Boat" during the 2022 Spring Festival, continuing through "Happy Chinese New Year and Seven Days" in 2023 and "From the River to the Sea" in 2025. These actions center on collective bodily practice, creating possibilities for gathering and connection in urban public space-carrying a boat through the streets, throwing wild parties and camping, paddling down the river-each action is a rehearsing of "being together" as the most fundamental political gesture.
