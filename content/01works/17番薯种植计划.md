date: 2017-09-04

番薯种植计划：在城市闲逛的时候把番薯种到某些公共空间/绿地里去。番薯是下贱容易生长的 作物,同时又有着某种身份认同的附带性，其种植和生长在这里是游击式的——城市漫游中的行为， 又可以慵懒地不管不顾这些分散式分布和随时收割的块茎乃至叶子。在几乎消灭了可食用种植作 物的城市空间中,是否有一些空隙可以进行游击种植?是否可以成为我们某种身体上的可能,，提 供给其他希望逃避统治的空间漫游者以选择?

*我们故意要在财富论坛那天到琶洲的会馆外面种番薯和野餐。但我没有想到找到一个合适的野餐 地点比想象中的艰难一点。但这并不是一个占领、被驱逐、继续寻找、占领的故事，而是如何在 一个大家都紧张兮兮得神智不清的时候，你仍需要保持神志清醒来玩耍的故事。......我微笑着眼 角浮现出了爱意，和那天的阳光一起躺在绿色的草坪，望着对面的高楼:“干嘛要工作，野餐才 是正经事啊!”* (引用自《在城市寻找野餐场所并嬉笑玩耍》，陈雅得，《等等目录》第二期番薯专号，2017)

Sweet potato planting project: planting sweet potatoes in some public space/ green space while wandering around the city. Sweet potato is a lowly, easy-to-grow crop with a certain identity attached to it, and its planting and growing here is guerrilla -- the act of roaming the city and lazily ignoring the decentralized distribution and ready-to-harvest tubers and even leaves. In an urban space where edible crops have almost been eliminated, are there gaps where guerrilla growing can take place? Could it be a physical possibility for us to offer options to other space roamers who wish to escape domination?

*We purposely wanted to plant potatoes and have a picnic outside the Pazhou venue on the day of the Fortune Forum. But I didn't realize that finding a suitable picnic spot would be a little tougher than I thought. But this isn't a story of occupation, eviction, continued search, occupation, but rather a story of how you need to stay sane to have fun when everyone is so nervous and delirious. ...... I smiled with love in the corners of my eyes, lying on the green lawn with the sun that day, looking at the tall buildings across the street: "Why work, picnics are the real thing!"* (Quoted from "Finding Picnic Places and Laughing and Playing in the City", Chen Yade, Etc. Etc. Catalogue, vol.02, 2017)
