顺流而下小组（龚豪、子杰和园子园）沿长江从武汉划船到上海，一路上邀请朋友来一起野餐，把不同的人卷入其中，全程1200公里涉水逾月。这是一次行动/事件，也是小组本身。

From the River to the Sea towards COOP(Gonghao, Zijie and Yuanziyuan), paddled down the Yangtze from Wuhan to Shanghai, 1200km over 31days. They invite peolple to picnic with them along the river and drawing various participants in to the fold. The journey is the event, and the event is the collective FRTS2COOP itself.
