我在东湖的湖心路路边发现一个临时搭建的或废弃的小棚屋，我决定干脆把小屋占用一段时间， 命名其为“东湖湖心大旅店”。我会放一些用品和使用指南在里面，游览东湖的人可以去歇息甚至 过夜。
By the side of Huxin Road along East Lake, I found a makeshift, abandoned shed. I decided to occupy it for a while and named it the **"East Lake Central Hotel."** I left some basic supplies and a user guide inside so that people visiting East Lake could rest there or even stay overnight.
more: http://donghu2010.org/?page_id=233
