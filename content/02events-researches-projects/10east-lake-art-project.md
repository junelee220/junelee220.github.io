东湖艺术计划是一个围绕武汉东湖展开的长期空间实践项目，始于2010年。通过一系列行为艺术、空间干预和社区参与，探讨城市化进程中的空间正义、公共权利和生态议题。这些行动以身体的在地介入为核心，在湖边的废墟、栈桥、渔场等日常空间中制造临时的公共时刻。

East Lake Art Project is a long-term spatial practice initiative centered around East Lake in Wuhan, beginning in 2010. Through a series of performance art, spatial interventions, and community engagement, it explores issues of spatial justice, public rights, and ecology in the process of urbanization. These actions center on embodied in-situ interventions, creating temporary public moments in the everyday spaces along the lake—ruins, piers, fish farms, and roadsides.
