子杰是一个漫画家、写作者、独立编辑和行动者；现居武汉，工作主要聚焦于城市化与空间正义、媒介考古（早期计算历史与文字处理技术）、战术媒体行动，以及以独立出版（Zine）作为自我管理的实践工具。
Zijie/ June LEE currently lives and works in Wuhan. He is an alternative comics maker, writer and activist who focuses on illustration and manga culture as an artistic tool within anti-gentrification movements, revolving especially around marginalised characters involved with issues of urbanisation and spatial justice. From 2010-2014, he co-organised Womenjia Youth Autonomy Lab (我们家青年自治实验室), an open house and social experiment located near the historic East Lake on the urban fringes of Wuhan. Now he and friends is running a alternative space “Fuyin.info” (复印 info).
