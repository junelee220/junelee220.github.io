“中文文字处理机：写作、权力、资本及技术”是子杰围绕中文计算技术、写作、权力和资本之间关系展开的长期研究项目。从2020年开始翻译 Matthew Kirschenbaum 的《追踪修订》为起点，陆续通过装置、表演性讲座和独立出版，探讨中文数字化历史及其对新自由主义中国现状的影响，以及写作工具变化对写作本身和人与技术关系的影响。

"Chinese Word Processing:writing, power, capital and technology" is a long-term research project by Zijie examining the relationship between Chinese computing technology, writing, power, and capital. Starting from 2020 with the translation of Matthew Kirschenbaum's *Track Changes*, it explores the history of Chinese digitization and its influence on the neoliberal present through installations, lecture performances, and independent publishing.
