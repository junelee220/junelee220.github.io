中文文字处理机是子杰围绕中文计算技术、写作、权力和资本之间关系展开的长期研究项目。从2020年开始翻译 Matthew Kirschenbaum 的《追踪修订》为起点，陆续通过装置、表演性讲座和独立出版，探讨中文数字化历史及其对新自由主义现状的影响，以及写作工具变化对写作本身和人与技术关系的影响。研究涉及四通公司打字机、中文输入法大众化、键盘写作的转向等关键节点。

Chinese Word Processing is a long-term research project by Zijie examining the relationship between Chinese computing technology, writing, power, and capital. Starting from 2020 with the translation of Matthew Kirschenbaum's *Track Changes*, it explores the history of Chinese digitization and its influence on the neoliberal present through installations, lecture performances, and independent publishing-how the shift from pen to keyboard transformed writing itself and the human-machine relationship. The research touches on key nodes such as the Stone typewriter, the popularization of Chinese input methods, and the turn to keyboard-based writing.
